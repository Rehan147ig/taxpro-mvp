# ASC 740 — Income Tax Provision Reference

## Core Concepts

### Permanent Differences
Items recognized differently for book and tax that **never reverse**:
- Meals & entertainment (50% non-deductible)
- Penalties & fines
- Tax-exempt interest income
- Life insurance proceeds (key-person)
- Dividends-received deduction
- Non-deductible goodwill impairment

### Temporary Differences
Items where timing differs but **eventually reverse**:
- Depreciation (book SL vs tax MACRS)
- Bad debt reserves (book accrual vs tax write-off)
- Warranty reserves
- Deferred revenue
- R&D capitalization (Section 174)
- NOL carryforwards
- Tax credit carryforwards

### No Difference
Accounts treated identically for book and tax:
- Cash and cash equivalents
- Accounts receivable/payable
- Revenue (generally)
- Salaries & wages
- Rent
- Utilities

## Tax Rate
- US Federal corporate rate: 21% (post-TCJA)
- State rates vary (0%–11.5%)
- State tax is deductible against federal income
