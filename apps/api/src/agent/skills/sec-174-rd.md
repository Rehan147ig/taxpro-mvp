# IRC Section 174 — Research & Experimental Expenditures

## Background
Effective for tax years beginning after December 31, 2021 (TCJA), Section 174 **requires capitalization** of research and experimental (R&E) expenditures — no longer immediately deductible.

## Key Rules
- **Domestic R&E**: Capitalized and amortized over **5 years** (straight-line, mid-year convention)
- **Foreign R&E**: Capitalized and amortized over **15 years** (straight-line, mid-year convention)
- Applies to: wages, supplies, contract research, and cloud computing costs for R&D

## Interaction with ASC 740
- Book treatment: R&D is typically expensed as incurred
- Tax treatment: Capitalized and amortized over 5/15 years
- **Creates a temporary difference** (deductible temporary → DTA)
- The DTA reverses as tax amortization catches up to book expense

## Section 41 R&D Tax Credit
- Separate from Section 174 capitalization
- 20% of qualified research expenses (QRE) over base amount (regular method)
- 14% of QRE over 50% of average QRE (alternative simplified method)
- Can offset up to 75% of tax liability (100% for startups under $5M revenue)
